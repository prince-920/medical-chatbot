Summary for your doctor

🚨 Emergency help

Call your local emergency number now (for example 112, 911 or 999, depending on your country) if someone:

  • is unresponsive or not breathing normally
  • has chest pain or pressure, or stroke signs (face droop, arm weakness, slurred speech)
  • is choking, has severe bleeding, a seizure, or a serious allergic reaction
  • is thinking about harming themselves

Stay on the line, give the exact location, and follow the dispatcher's instructions. Don't drive yourself if you suspect a heart attack or stroke.
